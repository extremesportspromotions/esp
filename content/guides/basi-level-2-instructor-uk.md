- **What it is:** BASI's second level, in ski or snowboard.
- **What you need first:** The BASI Level 1 qualification in the same discipline.
- **What you'll learn:** The Central Theme, the TIED teaching model, lesson planning and delivery, group management and safety. Ski adds bumps, steeps, variable snow and freestyle. Snowboard adds fitness and off-snow theory.
- **How long it takes:** Eight days on the mountain for the full Level 2, or four days for UK Level 2.
- **Cost:** BASI's Level 2 course fee is £900 from 1 June 2026. Lift passes aren't included.
- **What's next:** [BASI Level 3 ISIA](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/basi-level-3-isia-uk).

Bigger hills, more terrain and real ski school days. That's Level 2.

## Full Level 2 (8 days)

- **Ski:** you're assessed on teaching up to and including parallel skiing on marked pistes, within a ski school.
- **Snowboard:** you're assessed on teaching the Central Theme up to and including basic turning, and on instructing in a patrolled area.

## UK Level 2 (4 days)

This course builds on Level 1 and focuses on your teaching. By the end, you should be able to teach safely and effectively in a mountain or centre-based environment (for skiers, up to and including parallel skiing). Skiers who pass can convert to the full Level 2 through the four-day Ski Level 2 Re-assessment course.

## Re-assessment

If you fail one element of the full course (teaching or technical), there's a four-day re-assessment. Fail both and you retake the full course. For snowboarders, the original course must be within the last two years.

> [!SAFETY]
> On the mountain, stay inside the patrolled area unless you're qualified and equipped to leave it.
