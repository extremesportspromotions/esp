- **What it is:** BASI's international-standard qualification, built from separate modules.
- **What you need first:** The BASI Level 2 qualification in your main discipline.
- **What you'll learn:** Advanced teaching strategies, technical riding and performance analysis.
- **How long it takes:** A four-day Teaching course and a five-day Technical course, plus 200 hours of teaching experience.
- **Cost:** Not published on BASI's course pages. Lift passes aren't included.
- **What's next:** [BASI Level 4 ISTD](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/basi-level-4-istd-uk).

Seasons, not weekends. Level 3 is for instructors who want teaching to be their job.

## What it's built from

- **Level 3 Teaching (4 days):** learner-focused teaching and adapting your style.
- **Level 3 Technical (5 days):** your own riding or skiing, and performance analysis beyond the Central Theme.
- **200 hours of teaching:** part of the Level 3 ISIA pathway, ideally in a mountain environment. Skiers are strongly advised to finish them before the Teaching course. Snowboarders should have them done, or be working towards them, by then.
- **A second discipline:** the ISIA minimum standards ask for a Level 1 instructor course in another discipline, such as ski, snowboard, adaptive, telemark or cross-country.

Snowboarders on the Level 3 Technical course must bring a transceiver, avalanche shovel and probe, in a small rucksack.

> [!SAFETY]
> If you carry avalanche kit, know how to use it. Your trainer may ask you to carry it on days with possible off-piste.

Source: [Ski courses](https://basi.org.uk/courses/progress/ski) and [Snowboard courses](https://basi.org.uk/courses/progress/snowboard), BASI, checked October 2026.

Governing body: [Snowsport England](https://snowsportengland.org.uk) in England, [Snowsport Scotland](https://www.snowsportscotland.org) in Scotland and [Snowsport Cymru Wales](https://snowsportwales.com) in Wales. They set the standards for skiing and snowboarding, so they're the official places to find out more. Instructor qualifications also come from [BASI](https://basi.org.uk).
