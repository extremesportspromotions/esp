- **What it is:** The highest level in BASI's instructor system.
- **What you need first:** The BASI Level 3 qualification in your discipline. Ski Level 4 Teaching also needs 200 hours of teaching experience.
- **Cost:** Not published on BASI's course pages. Lift passes aren't included.
- **What's next:** [Coaching pathways](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/snowsport-coaching-pathways-uk).

This is the summit of BASI's system: the Level 4 International Snowsports Teaching Diploma.

## The courses

- **Level 4 Teaching (4 days):** high-level, learner-centred coaching. For snowboard, BASI calls it the final teaching module in its pathway. Skiers need 200 hours of teaching, ideally logged in the mountains between Level 3 Teaching and this course.
- **Level 4 Technical, ski (4 days):** short turns, long turns and bumps at the highest level, on a performance ski.
- **Level 4 Technical, snowboard (5 days):** high-end riding across every strand, with video feedback. Bring a transceiver, shovel and probe for the variable strand.
- **Level 4 Freeride, ski (3 days):** steeps and variable snow in lift-accessed off-piste, on a wider ski. Bring a transceiver, shovel and probe.

> [!SAFETY]
> BASI says Freeride isn't a mountain safety course. Be confident with your transceiver, shovel and probe before day one.

They set the standards for skiing and snowboarding, so they're the official places to find out more. Instructor qualifications also come from [BASI](https://basi.org.uk).
