- **What it is:** Licensed instructors train novices to CP level and beyond, at BHPA registered schools.
- **What you need first:** Annual Flying Membership, age 18+, your CFI's support, a Pilot rating, and 25 launches in the environment.
- **What you'll learn:** Every training exercise up to CP, plus BHPA operating procedures.
- **How long it takes:** At least 10 days of instructing experience, then an independent exam.
- **Cost:** Trainee Instructor registration is £40 (insurance included), the Instructor course is £450 (can vary with location), and the first Instructor exam is £50 (BHPA fees, 1 April 2026).
- **What's next:** Senior Instructor, then Instructor Training extension or Chief Flying Instructor.

Imagine watching your student's first flight leave the hill. Instructors make that happen, and the BHPA training is thorough.

## Before you start

- Be a BHPA Annual Flying Member.
- Be 18 or over.
- Be supported by the Chief Flying Instructor (CFI).
- Hold a Pilot rating in the discipline.
- Have 25 launches in the relevant environment (hill, tow or power).
- Register as a Trainee Instructor with the BHPA.

## The training programme

- Gain a recognised valid first aid certificate. For instructors that's at least 12 hours, including spinal injury, incident and casualty management.
- Attend a BHPA Coaching Course and a BHPA Instructor Course.
- Build up to at least 50 launches in the environment.
- Complete at least 10 days of instructing experience, with at least two days at each of two schools.
- Log at least 3 days as Duty Instructor, under supervision.
- Get signed off by the CFI on every exercise up to CP. The CFI runs a mock exam, then you pass an independent exam.

Once licensed, you can train a group of up to six students, or eight with a signed-off Trainee Instructor helping.

## Senior Instructor

- At least 25 days logged as a licensed Instructor, having taught every ab-initio exercise several times.
- Pass a BHPA Senior Instructor course and keep an Instructor Log Book.
- Get signed off by the CFI and pass an independent exam.

The Senior Instructor course is £200 a person (BHPA fees, 1 April 2026). With 50 days logged as a Senior Instructor, you can go for the Instructor Training extension. A school's Chief Flying Instructor (CFI) must hold a Senior Instructor licence.

Every school needs at least one Senior Instructor in each discipline it offers. Moving between paragliding and hang gliding counts as a new licence.

## Staying licensed

Licences are renewed every year with your membership. Your CFI checks that you have:

- At least 25 flights or 15 hours in the last 12 months.
- At least 10 days of instructing in the last 12 months, in each discipline and environment.
- A current first aid certificate.
- An Instructor Competence Assessment within the last 12 months.

> [!SAFETY]
> Emergency on a hill? Dial 999 or 112, ask for the Police, then Mountain Rescue. On the coast, ask for the Coastguard. That's from the [BHPA's emergency sheet](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/documents/index.php?doc=In_an_Emergency.pdf).
