- **What it is:** Flat-out sprints round a track of around 400 metres, packed with jumps and banked corners, up to eight riders at a time.
- **What you need first:** British Cycling membership. The provisional licence included with membership covers regional racing; national events need a full licence.
- **How long it takes:** A race lasts well under a minute, but a meeting is all-day action.
- **Cost:** Core membership is £52 a year, and a full race licence runs from no charge for under-16s to £40 for seniors (British Cycling, checked October 2026).
- **What's next:** Racing track: regional league, National Series, then the National and British Championships. Coaching track: [BMX coach](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/british-cycling-bmx-coach-uk). Volunteer track: timekeeper, judge or scrutineer.

## How a meeting works

It starts with qualifying heats called motos. Up to eight riders race each one, then the fastest move through knockout rounds to a final that decides the winner. Races are run off in quick succession, so it's non-stop for riders and spectators alike.

## The levels

British Cycling lists:

- regional racing leagues, with some regions running winter leagues;
- the Lloyds National BMX Race Series;
- the National Championships and the British Championships.

In 2026 the National Series ran ten rounds over five weekends from April to August, with stops in Manchester, Glasgow, Gosport, Cardiff and Derby. The British Championships at Platt Fields in Manchester drew 1,400 riders, from Superclass to veterans. Racing is family-friendly, often with several generations at one meeting.

## Supercross and time trial

Supercross is the Olympic discipline, raced over 350 to 400 metres, and the fastest qualifiers get the best spots on the start gate. In a time trial you ride one lap against the clock, either to seed riders or as a contest in its own right.

## Bikes and classes

Standard BMX bikes have 20-inch wheels, a single gear and usually just a rear brake. There are micro, mini and junior sizes for younger riders, and a 24-inch cruiser class for bigger or older ones.

## Wales and Scotland

Welsh Cycling (Beicio Cymru) runs its own Welsh BMX Championships. Scottish Cycling runs a BMX Racing Pathway from regional races to World Cups, with rider development guidance for riders, parents and coaches.

> [!SAFETY]
> In an emergency, call 999. British Cycling says full-face helmets are compulsory in BMX racing and full-finger gloves are a must. The NHS says call 999 if someone has hit their head and has been knocked out and not woken up, can't stay awake, has a fit, has fallen from more than 1 metre, has problems with walking, balance or speaking, or hit their head at high speed. That's from the NHS [head injury and concussion](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) page.
