What's on in the UK in 2027, sport by sport. Go and watch, or find out how to take part. Some dates are fixed, and some series haven't published their calendar yet.

> [!WARNING] Dates change
> Every date here is provisional, as of October 2026. Venues and dates can move, and weather can cancel outdoor events. Check the official calendar before you travel.

> [!SAFETY]
> To compete, you need the right licence, grade or membership for your sport. The governing body sets the entry rules for each event.

For who runs each sport, see [UK governing bodies by sport](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/uk-governing-bodies-by-sport).
