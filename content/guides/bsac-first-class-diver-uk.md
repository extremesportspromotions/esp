- **What it is:** BSAC's highest diver grade, for divers who can run safe but challenging expeditions in the UK and abroad.
- **What you need first:** Advanced Diver, 100 dives since qualifying (at least 20 deeper than 30m), and six Skill Development Courses (listed below).
- **What you'll learn:** Expedition planning, leading teams and diving theory at the highest level.
- **How long it takes:** There's no set programme. You prepare with mentors and preparation events.
- **Cost:** BSAC doesn't publish prices for the prep events or the exam.
- **What's next:** Diving track: expeditions and national roles. Instructing: First Class Diver is needed to go from Advanced Instructor to National Instructor (see [instructor grades](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bsac-instructor-grades-uk)).

## Before you start

1. Hold BSAC Advanced Diver.
2. Have logged 100 dives in a range of conditions since qualifying, at least 20 of them deeper than 30m.
3. Have passed these Skill Development Courses:
   - Chartwork and Position Fixing
   - Diver Coxswain Award
   - Oxygen Administration
   - Practical Rescue Management
   - Advanced Lifesaver
   - First Aid for Divers

## The exam

Three modules, assessed nationally. You can take them in any order:

- A theory knowledge exam.
- An advanced expedition plan.
- A two-day practical diving assessment, judged against 12 criteria including teamwork and leadership.

## How to begin

Register your interest with BSAC. The First Class Diver Chief Examiner can then give you general and specific advice. You'll need full open-water kit and access to some specialist equipment.

## Medical

A BSAC self-declaration medical form, done when you join and every year after. Answer yes to anything and you'll need to see a UKDMC medical referee.

> [!SAFETY]
> Suspect decompression illness or a lung injury? At sea, call the Coastguard on VHF DSC or Channel 16, or dial 999. Inland in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, call the BHA/RN Diver helpline on 07831 151523 (24 hours). Inland in Scotland, call Aberdeen Royal Infirmary on 0345 408 6008 (24 hours). That's from [BSAC's incident guidance](https://www.bsac.com/safety/diving-incidents/in-the-event-of-a-diving-incident/).
