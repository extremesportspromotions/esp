- **What it is:** BSAC's volunteer instructor ladder, from your first one-day course to National Instructor.
- **What you need first:** Sports Diver (or equivalent) and age 16 or over.
- **What you'll learn:** Teaching theory and open-water lessons, then dive management and training other instructors.
- **How long it takes:** The IFC and the Open Water Instructor Course are one day each, the Practical Instructor Exam about half a day, and Advanced Instructor a two-day course plus an exam.
- **Cost:** Not priced on BSAC's instructor pages. Your club or centre will tell you.
- **What's next:** Your diver grade sets how high you can go, so [Dive Leader](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bsac-dive-leader-advanced-diver-uk) and [First Class Diver](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bsac-first-class-diver-uk) unlock the top steps.

## The ladder

1. **Assistant Diving Instructor:** the one-day Instructor Foundation Course (IFC). Sports Diver, 16+.
2. **Theory Instructor:** a ten-minute classroom lesson plus a 60-question paper. IFC, Dive Leader, 18+.
3. **Assistant Open Water Instructor:** the one-day Open Water Instructor Course. IFC, Dive Leader, 16+.
4. **Practical Instructor:** a 45-minute open-water lesson plus a rescue drill. Dive Leader, IFC, Open Water Instructor Course, 18+.
5. **Open Water Instructor:** yours once steps 1 to 4 are done.
6. **Advanced Instructor:** for Open Water Instructors who are Advanced Divers. A two-day course, then a 60-question theory paper and two days of practical assessment.
7. **National Instructor:** for Advanced Instructors who are First Class Divers and Instructor Trainers, with a Pass or Merit in the First Class Diver theory exam in the last three years. It ends with a four-day national exam.

## Coming from another agency?

The Instructor Crossover is a BSAC Open Water Instructor Course. Bring BSAC membership, proof of your open-water instructor grade, an oxygen administration qualification and active teaching in the last 5 years (inactive longer, and you sit the Practical Instructor Exam too). It's for teaching in BSAC clubs.

## Paid instructing

When divers pay for training, the HSE says the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 apply: a diving project plan, and staff divers with an in-date HSE medical certificate. More in [other agencies and the HSE rules](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/padi-ssi-saa-raid-scuba-uk).

> [!SAFETY]
> Suspect decompression illness or a lung injury? At sea, call the Coastguard on VHF DSC or Channel 16, or dial 999. Inland in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, call the BHA/RN Diver helpline on 07831 151523 (24 hours). Inland in Scotland, call Aberdeen Royal Infirmary on 0345 408 6008 (24 hours). That's from [BSAC's incident guidance](https://www.bsac.com/safety/diving-incidents/in-the-event-of-a-diving-incident/).
