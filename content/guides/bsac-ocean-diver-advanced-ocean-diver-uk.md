- **What it is:** BSAC's entry-level grade (20m) and the optional next step (30m).
- **What you need first:** Ocean Diver: age 12 or over, able to swim 200m comfortably in a pool, and the medical form. Advanced Ocean Diver: Ocean Diver (or an equivalent from another agency plus Nitrox training) and age 14 or over.
- **What you'll learn:** Basic kit, buoyancy, gas planning, Nitrox up to 36%, then compass navigation and marker buoys.
- **How long it takes:** Around five days at a BSAC centre, or weeks to months at a club. Advanced Ocean Diver can take two days at a centre.
- **Cost:** A PADI or SSI Open Water course at a UK centre is usually £445 to £900. BSAC courses are run by clubs and centres, which set their own prices.
- **What's next:** Diving track: [Sports Diver](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bsac-sports-diver-uk). Instructing starts after Sports Diver. See [BSAC instructor grades](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bsac-instructor-grades-uk).

## Ocean Diver

- Six theory modules.
- At least five sessions in a pool or sheltered water.
- At least four open-water dives.
- A short theory exam, with continuous assessment throughout.

Once you qualify, you can dive to 20m with another Ocean Diver, under a Dive Manager, at places and in conditions you met in training. With a Dive Leader or above, you can try new places and conditions. You'll also be qualified to use Nitrox up to 36%. Rescue skills start on this course too.

The only kit you need to start is a swimsuit and a towel.

## Advanced Ocean Diver

It sits between Ocean Diver and Sports Diver, and it's optional. You'll do:

- Four theory sessions, in a classroom or online.
- At least two open-water sessions and two depth-progression dives, to 25m and then 30m.
- Compass navigation, SMB and DSMB use, more Nitrox, and two of the Sports Diver theory modules.

Once qualified, you can dive to 30m with another Advanced Ocean Diver, accompanied by a Sports Diver or above. Those dives must be no-decompression, in conditions you've met in training. You can still buddy an Ocean Diver, down to 20m.

## Medical

A BSAC self-declaration medical form, done when you join and every year after. Answer yes to anything and you'll need to see a UKDMC medical referee.

> [!SAFETY]
> Suspect decompression illness or a lung injury? At sea, call the Coastguard on VHF DSC or Channel 16, or dial 999. Inland in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, call the BHA/RN Diver helpline on 07831 151523 (24 hours). Inland in Scotland, call Aberdeen Royal Infirmary on 0345 408 6008 (24 hours). That's from [BSAC's incident guidance](https://www.bsac.com/safety/diving-incidents/in-the-event-of-a-diving-incident/).
