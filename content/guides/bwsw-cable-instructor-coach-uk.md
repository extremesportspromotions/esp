- **What you need first:** BWSW membership, age 16 or over (17 to be qualified or licensed), and the riding standard for each level (below).
- **What you'll learn:** Safety practices, risk assessment, customer service, delivery skills and wakeboard technique.
- **How long it takes:** Blended learning: an online part, then a practical course. Cable Coach Level 2 is 6 hours online plus 2 days on the water.
- **Cost:** For Cable Instructor, BWSW says to ask your local cable centre or employer. Cable Coach Level 2 is £350, plus a £48 coach licence fee (BWSW, checked October 2026). BWSW's two fee tables label the £200 one-day course differently, so ask BWSW which level it covers.
- **What's next:** Coaching track: Instructor > Level 1 > Level 2. Operating: [cable operator](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bwsw-cable-operator-uk), which most centres expect too. Riding: [Progressive Edge](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/progressive-edge-wakeboard-awards-uk).

## Cable Instructor

For anyone instructing beginners at a wake park. You'll be able to teach a rider's first sessions, covering everything in Progressive Edge Bronze: a land lesson, deep-water start (straight-line) or dock start (full cable), riding position, heel and toe edging, and corners.

Before the course you need BWSW membership, to be 16 or over, and to be able to do every skill on the Wake Park Progressive Edge Silver Award.

## Cable Coach Level 1

For coaching beginner to intermediate riders, up to Progressive Edge Silver: surface rotations, switch riding, ollies and first feature tricks.

Before the course you need BWSW membership, the Cable Instructor qualification, to be 16 or over, and every skill on the Wake Park Progressive Edge Gold Award (Stage 2).

## Cable Coach Level 2

For coaching beginner to advanced riders, up to Progressive Edge Gold: advanced kicker and rail tricks, and aerial tricks.

Before the course you need BWSW membership, Cable Coach Level 1, to be 16 or over, and every skill on the Wake Park Progressive Edge Gold Award.

## For every level

- If you can't show the riding standard on the course, you'll need video or references from licensed Level 2 coaches.
- BWSW doesn't insist on an operating qualification, but says you're unlikely to work at a centre without operating too.
- Before you're certified, BWSW needs you to hold a first aid certificate from a full-day, in-person course (online courses aren't accepted), a Safeguarding and Protecting Children certificate (BWSW runs one online), and an Enhanced Disclosure (DBS) check.

> [!SAFETY]
> BWSW's safety recommendations say every site should have an emergency action plan that all staff understand and ready access for the emergency services, and suggest the what3words app to confirm your location. At the coast, the RNLI says call 999 and ask for the Coastguard. If you're struggling in the water, Float to Live: tilt your head back with your ears under, relax, control your breathing, and move your hands and legs to stay afloat. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/safety/beach-safety).
