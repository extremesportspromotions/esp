- **What it is:** Coaching awards focused on how climbers move, rather than on keeping a group safe.
- **What you need first:** Foundation Coach: you must be 16, have 6 months of climbing on a variety of walls and be a member of the BMC, Mountaineering Scotland or Mountaineering Ireland. Development Coach needs Foundation Coach training. Performance Coach needs the Development Coach award.
- **What you'll learn:** Coaching movement, from the fundamentals up to long-term plans for individual climbers.
- **How long it takes:** Foundation Coach: 1 day of training and a 2-hour assessment. Development Coach: 2 days of training and a 4-hour assessment. Performance Coach: 8 days plus online sessions over 3 to 4 months.
- **Cost:** Registration is £69 for Foundation and Development Coach, and £103 for Performance Coach. Course prices are set by providers.
- **What's next:** [Climbing competitions](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/climbing-competitions-uk) (climbing track)

Instructors keep people safe. Coaches make them better.

## Foundation Coach

A Foundation Coach helps new and improving climbers with the fundamentals of movement, usually with groups and sometimes under a more experienced Development Coach. It suits wall staff, outdoor instructors and young people who've completed NICAS Level 4 or 5.

- Before training: Mountain Training strongly recommends the FUNdamentals of Climbing 1 workshop.
- Before assessment: the FUNdamentals of Climbing 1 workshop, at least 15 coaching sessions logged since training, reflective notes on at least five of them, and lesson plans ready to show the assessor.

You'll also need a Safeguarding Children in Sport module, and to be experienced and qualified enough to run safe sessions wherever you coach.

## Development Coach

For coaches who work with groups over a series of sessions, or with individuals keen to improve. You must be 18, with 12 months of personal climbing.

- Before training: Foundation Coach training (or direct entry through Mountain Training's Accredited Prior Learning process), and the FUNdamentals of Climbing 1 and 2 workshops.
- Before assessment: 40 coaching sessions logged since training, reflective comments on at least 10, progressive lesson plans, a home paper and a safeguarding module.

## Performance Coach

The top level, for experienced coaches helping climbers reach mid to long-term goals. Registration is an application, and each yearly training group has a maximum of 12 places.

- To register: the Development Coach award, onsight climbs up to 7a, over 200 hours of logged coaching, FUNdamentals of Climbing 1, 2 and 3 and Physical Training 1, current first aid and safeguarding training, and a DLOG reference.
- Assessment: a portfolio, an interview and an observed coaching session.

> [!SAFETY]
> Good coaching starts with a safe session. Don't push a climber's movement before their ropework is solid.
