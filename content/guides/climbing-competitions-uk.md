- **What it is:** Indoor competition climbing in bouldering, lead and speed.
- **What you need first:** BMC membership for the Youth Climbing Series and the England and Wales Climbing Trials.
- **What you'll learn:** How to climb under pressure, on routes you've never seen.
- **How long it takes:** A season at a time.
- **Cost:** Entry fees vary by event.
- **What's next:** [Kit, costs and safety](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/mountaineering-kit-costs-safety-uk)

Chalk in the air, a crowd behind you and one shot at the problem. Competition climbing is pure focus.

## Where to start

- **BMC Youth Climbing Series:** regional rounds across England and Wales, then a two-day Grand Final for the top three in each category from each region (U11s don't go to the final). First-timers are welcome. The 2026 categories run from U11 to U19.
- **England and Wales Climbing Trials:** the selection event for the England Climbing Foundation and Development programmes, for U13 and U15 climbers.

## The national calendar

The BMC lists the British Boulder, Lead, Speed, Para Climbing and Ice Climbing Championships, plus the British Youth Boulder and Lead Championships. For 2027 dates, see our [British competitions 2027](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/british-competitions-2027) guide and the BMC's national competition calendar.

> [!SAFETY]
> Warm up properly. Competition problems are built to push you, and cold fingers and shoulders get hurt.
