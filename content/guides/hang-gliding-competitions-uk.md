- **What it is:** Cross-country racing and leagues for hang glider pilots, run under the BHPA's hang gliding competitions panel.
- **What you need first:** Normally the Pilot rating, because that's what lets you fly cross-country under BHPA rules. At BHPA-sanctioned events, Club Pilots can fly limited corridor tasks after a documented briefing. Each event sets its own entry rules.
- **What you'll learn:** Task flying, retrieves and how your flying stacks up against other pilots.
- **How long it takes:** British Open Series rounds run for about eight days, with part-week entry options.
- **Cost:** The panel prices each round, with a lower early-bird fee and part-week options. Prices come out with each round's information.
- **What's next:** Pilot track: international events such as the Class 1 Worlds. Coaching track: [Club Coach](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/hang-gliding-coaches-instructors-uk).

## Who runs it

The BHPA Competitions Committee includes a hang gliding panel. The panel runs the national championships, picks national teams and oversees other hang gliding competitions. The BHPA lists three places to start:

- **[British Hang Gliding Competitions](https://hgcomps.uk)**, the panel's own site, with news, results and entry details.
- **UK National XC League:** BHPA members can enter their cross-country flights. The BHPA links it from its [competitions page](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/sport/competitions).
- **The British Club Challenge.**

## The British Open Series

The British Open Series (BOS) welcomes every hang glider pilot, not just experts. It offers development help based on the panel's Pilot Development Pathway for new and returning pilots, plus workshops, presentations and social events on days you can't fly.

The series runs as rounds of about eight days at UK flying sites, such as the Yorkshire Dales and Mid Wales, with scoring tasks counted across the season. Pilots can earn FAI world ranking points. The panel holds its planning meeting in November, so look out for 2027 dates on its site after that.

## The UK Nationals

The panel runs the UK Nationals, a week or so of tasks that can be held abroad alongside another country's championships. Watch [hgcomps.uk](https://hgcomps.uk) for the next venue and dates.

## Abroad

> [!SAFETY]
> Emergency on the hill? Dial 999 or 112 and ask for the Police, then Mountain Rescue. On the coast, ask for the Coastguard. That's the advice on the [BHPA's emergency sheet](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/documents/index.php?doc=In_an_Emergency.pdf).
