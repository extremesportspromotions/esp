- **What you need first:** BKSA membership for British events. The GKA's minimum age is 14.
- **What's next:** The GKA Kite World Tour.

## At home

- BKSA runs the national competition schedule and crowns the UK champions. The 2026 series brought together juniors, amateurs and pros from the UK kite and wing community.
- Events cover Big Air, Foiling Festivals and Wave Masters.
- Big Air has Open and Amateur fleets. Amateur is for non-sponsored riders and is judged on overall impression.
- Entry is open to all BKSA members. Register online by 7pm the night before, because there are no entries on the day.
- Judges score height, risk, technical difficulty and innovation.
- Impact vests, helmets and kite safety leashes are mandatory.
- BKSA says the series is a springboard to the GKA Kite World Tour.

Dates for next year are in our [British competitions 2027 guide](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/british-competitions-2027).

## The world tour

- The GKA Kite World Tour runs four official disciplines, all sanctioned by World Sailing: Freestyle, Kitesurf, Big Air and Hydrofoil Big Air.
- There are around 15 World Cups a year.
- Top-ranked riders qualify automatically. You can also get in by video application or a local wildcard.
- Entry is €125 a discipline. GKA membership is mandatory: €80 for one event or €160 for the year.

> [!SAFETY]
> Always kite with another person. Never ride out further than you can swim back. In trouble at the coast? Call 999 or 112 and ask for the coastguard. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/safety/choose-your-activity/kitesurfing).
