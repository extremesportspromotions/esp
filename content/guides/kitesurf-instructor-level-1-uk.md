- **What it is:** The British Kitesports (BKSA) entry-level award for teaching kitesurfing.
- **What you need first:** BKSA membership, 12 hours of shadow teaching, verified riding, RYA Powerboat Level 2 and an in-person first aid course.
- **What you'll learn:** The BKSA syllabus, plus foil and single-skin kites, extra safety, and the legal side of setting up a UK school.
- **How long it takes:** A 5-day course, then a post-course action plan.
- **Cost:** Not listed on BKSA's training page. Ask when you book.
- **What's next:** [Level 2, Senior Instructor and beyond](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/kitesurf-senior-instructor-uk). Prefer land? [Powerkite instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/powerkite-instructor-uk).

Turn your riding into a job on the water. BKSA built one recognised instructor training route for kitesurfing in the UK, and this is the first step on it.

## Prerequisites

- Valid British Kitesports membership.
- 12 hours of shadow teaching.
- Riding competency verified by a British Kitesports instructor.
- RYA Powerboat Level 2.
- A valid first aid certificate from at least a 1-day attended first aid course. Online courses aren't accepted.

## After the course

- You get a completion certificate.
- Meet your post-course action plan and you're certified as a full instructor.
- You can then teach within a British Kitesports recognised school.
- Once registered, you can teach up to 4 students with two kites on the water.
- You can also teach intermediate skills: equipment tuning, technique, riding upwind, transitions and jumping.

The training is modular, so you can build up to the full award over time, with prior experience and qualifications counted.

> [!SAFETY]
> Always kite with another person. Never ride out further than you can swim back. In trouble at the coast? Call 999 or 112 and ask for the coastguard. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/safety/choose-your-activity/kitesurfing).

Source: [Instructor Training – Water](https://britishkitesports.org/instructor-training/instructor-training-water/), British Kitesports, and [Kitesurfing safety](https://rnli.org/safety/choose-your-activity/kitesurfing), RNLI, checked October 2026.
