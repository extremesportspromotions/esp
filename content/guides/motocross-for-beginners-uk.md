Motocross is racing on a purpose-built off-road track, with jumps and fast corners [1]. Before a race, the ACU says coaching or a track day is a good idea, and the AMCA says a practice track is a good start for a beginner [2][4].

## A first session

The Auto-Cycle Union is the national governing body for motorcycle sport in the British Isles, apart from Northern Ireland [3].

Before you compete, the ACU says it is a good idea to do some coaching or training, and to ride a test day or a track day [2]. It has coaches and training providers who can help with those first experiences [2].

The Amateur Motor Cycle Association organises motocross racing [4]. It says riders should already have some experience on a track, and that a practice track is a good start for a beginner [4].

From 1 January 2026, an ACU junior may compete in the 50cc Automatic class from their 5th birthday, and not before [5]. The AMCA accepts motocross riders from the age of 7 [6]. At an ACU event, a youth rider should be able to sit astride the bike and, with one foot firmly on the ground, use the gear lever or the foot brake with the other foot [7].

## What to take

For an ACU event, take an ACU-approved motocross helmet that fits, chest and back protection, motocross boots, motocross kit, and gloves [1][7]. At the start of a race, wear goggles or a visor that will not splinter [7]. A kidney belt, knee braces and a neck brace are a personal choice [1].

The AMCA says you bring your own bike [6].

> [!SAFETY]
> A parent or legal guardian must go with a rider under 18, and stay for the whole time that rider is there [7]. They must make sure a young rider's protective clothing fits [7].
