Motocross is racing on a purpose-built off-road track, with jumps and fast corners. Before a race, the ACU says coaching or a track day is a good idea, and the AMCA says a practice track is a good start for a beginner.

## A first session

The Auto-Cycle Union is the national governing body for motorcycle sport in the British Isles, apart from Northern Ireland.

Before you compete, the ACU says it is a good idea to do some coaching or training, and to ride a test day or a track day. It has coaches and training providers who can help with those first experiences.

The Amateur Motor Cycle Association organises motocross racing. It says riders should already have some experience on a track, and that a practice track is a good start for a beginner.

From 1 January 2026, an ACU junior may compete in the 50cc Automatic class from their 5th birthday, and not before. The AMCA accepts motocross riders from the age of 7. At an ACU event, a youth rider should be able to sit astride the bike and, with one foot firmly on the ground, use the gear lever or the foot brake with the other foot.

## What to take

For an ACU event, take an ACU-approved motocross helmet that fits, chest and back protection, motocross boots, motocross kit, and gloves. At the start of a race, wear goggles or a visor that will not splinter. A kidney belt, knee braces and a neck brace are a personal choice.

The AMCA says you bring your own bike.

> [!SAFETY]
> A parent or legal guardian must go with a rider under 18, and stay for the whole time that rider is there. They must make sure a young rider's protective clothing fits.
