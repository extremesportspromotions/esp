- **What it is:** The award for leading groups in wild mountain terrain in the UK and Ireland.
- **What you need first:** You must be 18, have a year of mountain walking behind you and be a member of the BMC, Mountaineering Scotland or Mountaineering Ireland.
- **What you'll learn:** Planning and leading journeys in wild mountain terrain, and making the right decisions there.
- **How long it takes:** 6 days of training (including a 2-day expedition with a camp) and 5 days of assessment (including a 3-day expedition with two camps). Mountain Training says the average time from training to passing is 3.3 years.
- **Cost:** Registration is £69. A typical UK course costs £450 to £1,000, with Mountain Leader training and assessment at the top of that range.
- **What's next:** [Mountaineering and Climbing Instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/mountaineering-and-climbing-instructor-uk) (walking track)

Big hills, real weather and a group trusting you to bring them home. The Mountain Leader award is the classic UK walking qualification, and it takes years of genuine mountain days to earn.

## What you need

- Before training: 20 quality mountain days.
- Before assessment: 40 quality mountain days in at least three regions of the UK or Ireland, and at least 8 nights of camping, including 4 nights of wild camping, and a current first aid certificate from an in-person course of at least two full days (16 hours) with an assessment.

Log your days on DLOG, Mountain Training's digital logbook.

## Where it leads

Mountain Training also offers the Winter Mountain Leader and International Mountain Leader awards. The Mountain Leader is also the entry ticket for the [Mountaineering and Climbing Instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/mountaineering-and-climbing-instructor-uk) award.

> [!SAFETY]
> Mountain weather changes fast. Use a mountain forecast, and in an emergency dial 999 or 112 and ask for Police, then Mountain Rescue.
