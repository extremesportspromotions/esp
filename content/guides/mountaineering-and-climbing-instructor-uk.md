- **What it is:** Mountain Training's senior summer award for instructing climbing and mountaineering.
- **What you need first:** The Mountain Leader award, plus logged mountain days and multi-pitch and sport climbs (see below).
- **What you'll learn:** Instructing on big mountain crags, multi-pitch routes and in the mountains.
- **How long it takes:** Two 5-day training courses, then a 5-day assessment. Mountain Training says the average time from training to passing is 4.6 years.
- **Cost:** Registration is £103. Training and assessment prices are set by providers.
- **What's next:** [Kit, costs and safety](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/mountaineering-kit-costs-safety-uk). This is where the walking and climbing tracks meet.

Multi-pitch routes, mountain crags and sea cliffs: this is serious terrain, and the award asks for serious experience.

## To register

- Hold the Mountain Leader award.
- 20 quality mountain days as a Mountain Leader, in a variety of mountain areas with a variety of groups.
- 30 named multi-pitch rock climbs in at least three main climbing areas of the UK and Ireland, leading at least the crux pitch at VS 4c or above.
- 10 led sport climbs at French 6a or above.
- A reference on DLOG.

Registration is an application: Mountain Training reviews your logbook (this can take up to 30 days) and must approve it before you can book training. Courses are run by Glenmore Lodge and Plas y Brenin.

## Before assessment

After the second training course:

- 20 multi-pitch climbs at VS 4c or above, many of them on mountain crags and major sea cliffs.
- 20 quality mountain days in sole charge of a group.
- 20 rock climbing teaching days with a variety of climbers and venues.
- A current first aid certificate from an in-person course of at least two full days (16 hours) with an assessment.

These are the current rules. Mountain Training has agreed changes to the prerequisites, coming in between June 2026 and January 2028, so check its page before you register. Above this sits the Winter Mountaineering and Climbing Instructor award.

> [!SAFETY]
> On multi-pitch ground, retreat is part of the plan. Know your escape before you need it.
