The hills don't care how fit you are. Good habits do the heavy lifting.

## Typical UK prices

- A coached coasteering session or an outdoor intro climb: £55 to £150 per person.
- Leader and instructor training plus assessment: £450 to £1,000.
- An outdoor coach: £40 to £70 an hour.

## Membership

Most Mountain Training leader, instructor and coach awards ask you to be a member of a mountaineering council (the BMC, Mountaineering Scotland or Mountaineering Ireland) before you register. Registration is £69 for most awards, and £103 for Mountaineering and Climbing Instructor and Performance Coach.

## The law for under-18s

In England, Wales and Scotland, anyone who sells adventure activities to under-18s must hold a licence from the Adventure Activities Licensing scheme. That covers climbing, abseiling and scrambling outdoors (not on purpose-built walls or towers) and trekking in remote moorland or terrain over 600m. Schools teaching their own pupils, voluntary clubs with their own members, and young people with a parent or guardian don't need one.

> [!SAFETY]
> Use a mountain forecast, tell someone your route and turn back early. In an emergency, dial 999 or 112 and ask for Police, then Mountain Rescue.
