A tuned bike, a good helmet and honest decisions. That's most of it.

## Typical UK prices

- A coached booking: £80 to £250 per person. A trail-centre skills day is about £80, and a private full day is £175 to £250.
- A multi-day skills block: typically £250 to £600. There's no licence just to ride.
- British Cycling's own listed award prices: Off-Road Leader £205 to £275; Trail Leader £370 to £550 plus £250 to £350 assessment; Mountain Bike Leader £550 to £750 plus £400 to £550 assessment; Introduction to Coaching – Off-Road £270; Coaching in Context – MTB XC £415.
- Leaders and coaches also need first aid (two-day outdoor for leaders and Coaching in Context) and safeguarding, which aren't included.
- Private skills coaching: £45 to £70 an hour.

## Who does what

- **British Cycling, Scottish Cycling and Welsh Cycling:** share the off-road leadership awards (Off-Road Leader, Trail Leader and Mountain Bike Leader). British Cycling runs the coaching pathway and national racing.
- **Old Scottish MBLA awards:** British Cycling says MBLA courses are no longer available, but existing Trail Cycle Leader and MBL qualifications are still recognised.

> [!SAFETY]
> Check your brakes, tyres and quick releases before every ride, and ride trails that match your skills.
