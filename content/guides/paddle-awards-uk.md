- **What it is:** The UK's personal paddling awards, from your first go to discipline skills.
- **What you need first:** Nothing. No prerequisites, and they're open to all ages.
- **What you'll learn:** Confidence on sheltered water, then skills and decisions in the craft you choose.
- **Cost:** A lake taster or half-day guided paddle usually costs £35 to £90.
- **What's next:** Skills track: [Safety and rescue](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/paddlesport-safety-rescue-uk). Teaching track: [Paddlesport Instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/paddlesport-instructor-uk).

No registration needed. You pick the award that suits you, and you go at your own pace.

## The Paddle Awards

- **Paddle Start:** your first time on the water, having a go.
- **Paddle Discover:** the next step towards paddling on your own, building decision making and practical skills.
- **Paddle Explore:** you choose where you go next in paddlesport.

They're for people getting into a boat for the first time, on sheltered water. Instructors, coaches, leaders, bell boat helms and raft guides can all deliver them.

## Personal Performance Awards

These build your skills and decisions in the craft and environment you choose, whether that's freestyle tricks, polo or coastal sea kayaking. There's a range of awards in each discipline:

- Canoe, Freestyle, Polo, Racing, Rafting
- Sea Kayak, Slalom, SUP, Surf Kayak
- Touring, White Water, Wild Water Racing

Coaches and leaders deliver them. Find a course through your home nation's Delivery Centre.

Source: [Paddle Awards](https://britishcanoeingawarding.org.uk/paddle-awards/) and [Personal Performance Awards](https://britishcanoeingawarding.org.uk/personal-performance-awards/), British Canoeing Awarding Body, checked October 2026.

Governing body: [Paddle UK](https://paddleuk.org.uk). It sets the safety standards and training awards for paddlesport in England, so it's the official place to find out more.

The [club map](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/?sport=kayaking#find-a-club) lists some of the UK clubs for this sport. It is a starting point, so check with the club before you travel. To have a coach found for you, use the [enquiry form](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/#enquire).
