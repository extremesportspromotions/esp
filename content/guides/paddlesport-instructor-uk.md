- **What it is:** The first step to teaching. You run taster and starter sessions on very sheltered water.
- **What you need first:** [Paddlesport Safety and Rescue](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/paddlesport-safety-rescue-uk) (or Foundation Safety and Rescue Training) and full National Association membership. Minimum age 14.
- **What you'll learn:** Kitting up a group, getting afloat, games and mini journeys.
- **How long it takes:** 2 days of combined training and assessment.
- **Cost:** About £220 to £225, fees included.
- **What's next:** Coaching track: [Coach Award](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/coach-award-paddlesport-uk). Leading track: [White Water Leader](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/white-water-leader-uk).

It's built to get you "primed and ready" for your first instructing job, running sessions that are fun, safe and rewarding.

## Prerequisites

- Paddlesport Safety and Rescue, or a Foundation Safety and Rescue Training course.
- Full National Association membership.
- Aged 14 or older.
- Register with your Delivery Centre before your course.

Already have the skills? You can apply for Accredited Prior Learning to skip the safety training prerequisite.

## Where you can work

You work within the safety systems of a club, centre or other organisation:

- On very sheltered water.
- In craft suitable for a first-time experience.

## The assessment

Three tasks during the course:

- Professional standards
- Rescue skills
- Personal skills

The optional Instructor eLearning comes with registration.

Source: [Paddlesport Instructor](https://britishcanoeingawarding.org.uk/level-1-paddlesport-instructor/) and [Registration Requirements](https://britishcanoeingawarding.org.uk/registration-requirements/), British Canoeing Awarding Body, checked October 2026.

Governing body: [Paddle UK](https://paddleuk.org.uk). It sets the safety standards and training awards for paddlesport in England, so it's the official place to find out more.

The [club map](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/?sport=kayaking#find-a-club) lists some of the UK clubs for this sport. It is a starting point, so check with the club before you travel. To have a coach found for you, use the [enquiry form](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/#enquire).
