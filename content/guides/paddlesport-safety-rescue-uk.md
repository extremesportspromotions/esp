- **What it is:** Rescue courses for paddlers. One for sheltered water, one for white water.
- **What you need first:** Paddle Explore (or equal ability) for sheltered water. The matching Personal Performance Award (or equal ability) for white water.
- **How long it takes:** 6 hours for sheltered water. 2 days for white water.
- **Cost:** Coached time usually costs £35 to £60 an hour.
- **What's next:** Skills track: more [Personal Performance Awards](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/paddle-awards-uk). Leading track: [Paddlesport Instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/paddlesport-instructor-uk) or [White Water Leader](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/white-water-leader-uk).

Both are open to all ages, and both are practical courses where you practise the rescues for real. You get an attendance certificate from your Delivery Centre on the tutor's recommendation.

## Paddlesport Safety and Rescue

For every craft, on sheltered water. Six modules:

- Clothing and equipment
- Safety frameworks
- Contact and towing rescues
- Self-rescues
- Deep water rescues
- Reflection and development

It's also a prerequisite for the Paddlesport Instructor.

## White Water Safety

For being an independent paddler on grade 2(3) rivers. Nine modules over 2 days, including:

- River trip emergency planning and procedures
- Chase boating and equipment rescues
- Swimming and rescues
- Retrieving people and equipment
- Scenarios

It's one of the safety awards accepted for [White Water Leader](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/white-water-leader-uk) and [Raft Guide](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/raft-guide-uk) (Grade 2).

> [!SAFETY]
> In trouble at the coast? Call 999 or 112 and ask for the coastguard. Inland, on a river or lake, ask for Fire and Rescue. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/water-safety/how-to-call-for-help-at-sea).
