## Who the HSE approves

For divers working on recreational diving projects, the HSE's approved list (January 2026) includes BSAC, PADI, the Sub-Aqua Association (SAA), SCUBA Schools International UK (SSI), the Rebreather Association of International Divers (RAID AB) and the Scottish Sub-Aqua Club.

## PADI

- **Open Water Diver:** from age 10. You'll need to swim 200m (or 300m with mask, fins and snorkel) and float for 10 minutes.
- **Divemaster:** PADI's first pro level. Age 18+, Rescue Diver (or qualifying certification), Emergency First Response Primary and Secondary Care in the last 24 months, a physician's medical clearance in the last 12 months, and 40 logged dives to start (60 to certify).
- **Instructor (IDC):** a Divemaster who has been a certified diver for six months, with 60 logged dives (100 for the Instructor Examination), EFR training in the last 24 months, a medical in the last 12 months, and an EFR Instructor rating, which you can earn during training.

## SAA

The SAA calls itself the CMAS issuing authority in the UK, run by its members as an association of independent clubs. The UKDMC advises it, along with BSAC and the Scottish Sub-Aqua Club, on fitness to dive.

## How grades line up with BSAC

- **Ocean Diver:** PADI, SSI and RAID Open Water (or Advanced Open Water), and SAA Open Water.
- **Sports Diver:** PADI and RAID Rescue Diver, SAA Club Diver, and SSI Advanced Open Water with "Stress & Rescue".
- **Dive Leader:** PADI and RAID Divemaster, SAA Dive Leader and SSI Dive Control Specialist, each with an oxygen administration qualification.

## Paying for a dive

If you pay an instructor or guide, the Diving at Work Regulations 1997 apply. The HSE says expect a diving project plan you're briefed on, at least three named roles (a supervisor, a person on the surface and a safety or rescue diver), first aid and oxygen arrangements, and staff divers with an in-date HSE medical certificate. If nobody is at work, the rules don't apply, and the HSE recommends following your training agency's guidance.

> [!SAFETY]
> Suspect decompression illness or a lung injury? At sea, call the Coastguard on VHF DSC or Channel 16, or dial 999. Inland in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, call the BHA/RN Diver helpline on 07831 151523 (24 hours). Inland in Scotland, call Aberdeen Royal Infirmary on 0345 408 6008 (24 hours). That's from [BSAC's incident guidance](https://www.bsac.com/safety/diving-incidents/in-the-event-of-a-diving-incident/).
