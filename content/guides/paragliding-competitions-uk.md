- **What it is:** BHPA-supported cross-country competitions, from logging your own XC flights to the British Championships.
- **What you need first:** BHPA membership. The BP Cup needs the Pilot rating or above and an EN A, B or C glider.
- **What you'll learn:** Setting up your instruments, flying set tasks and flying alongside other pilots.
- **How long it takes:** The 2027 BP Cup Dales round runs over four days, 6 to 9 May.
- **Cost:** Entry fees aren't on the BHPA or BP Cup pages, so ask the organiser when you enter.
- **What's next:** Competition track: the British Championships and the Paragliding World Cup. Coaching track: [Club Coach](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/bhpa-club-coach-uk).

## Who runs it

The BHPA Competitions Committee supports free flight competitions. Its paragliding panel organises the national championships, selects the national teams and oversees all other paragliding competitions.

## Where to start

- **UK National XC League:** BHPA members enter their own cross-country flights.
- **British Club Challenge:** the club competition on the BHPA's list.
- **British Paragliding Cup:** a friendly UK competition for pilots in the "non-skygod" category. Its aims are "flying, fun and learning", in that order. The Meet Director handles the forecast, site and task, so you just take off and fly. You get help programming your instruments, and there are daily task debriefs when time allows.
- **British Paragliding Competitions:** the BHPA panel's site, home of the British Championships and the British Winter Open.

## 2027 dates

- **22 February to 2 March 2027:** 8th British Winter Open, Panchgani, India. It's open to all classes of wing.
- **6 to 9 May 2027:** BP Cup Dales round, Yorkshire Dales, with registration on the evening of Wednesday 5 May.

See the [2027 British competitions guide](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/british-competitions-2027) for every sport's events in one place.

## Going global

The BHPA lists the CIVL paragliding competitions page and the Paragliding World Cup.

## Cross-country rules

You need the Pilot rating to fly cross-country. The one competition exception is a BHPA-sanctioned event where the organisers set a defined corridor to goal and give Club Pilots a documented briefing.

> [!SAFETY]
> Emergency on a hill? Dial 999 or 112, ask for the Police, then Mountain Rescue. On the coast, ask for the Coastguard. That's from the [BHPA's emergency sheet](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/documents/index.php?doc=In_an_Emergency.pdf).
