- **What it is:** The award for guiding rafts on grade 2, 3 or 4 natural white water rivers. Paddle UK also runs rafting as a race sport.
- **What you need first:** Membership, a 2-day first aid award, safeguarding training and a [white water safety award](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/paddlesport-safety-rescue-uk). Minimum age 16.
- **What you'll learn:** Leadership, personal paddling and rescue skills.
- **How long it takes:** Optional training (normally 2 to 5 days), then a 1 to 2 day assessment.
- **Cost:** Your Delivery Centre lists course fees. A half-day white-water rafting trip sits in the same price band as a kayak taster, usually £35 to £90.
- **What's next:** Guiding track: Raft Trip Leader. Racing track: domestic races and GB team selection.

## Raft Guide prerequisites

- Full National Association membership.
- A valid (within 3 years) British Canoeing Awarding Body recognised 2-day First Aid Award.
- Valid (within 3 years) recognised safeguarding training.
- Grade 2: White Water Safety, White Water Safety and Rescue Training, or Rescue 3 WRT.
- Grade 3/4: Advanced White Water Safety, Advanced White Water Safety and Rescue Training, or Rescue 3 WRT Pro.
- Aged 16 or over at assessment.

Training is optional (Awarding Body courses usually run 2 to 5 days), and there's free Leadership eLearning.

The Stadium Raft Guide covers man-made white water courses, with the same membership, first aid and safeguarding prerequisites. Raft Trip Leaders need a 3-day first aid award, an advanced white water safety award and a Raft Guide award: Grade 3 or 4 to lead Grade 3 trips, Grade 4 to lead Grade 4.

## Raft racing

Paddle UK calls rafting a team sport for crews of 4 (R4) or 6 (R6), with domestic races through the year and a GB team selection policy for 2027. Race formats:

- **Slalom:** gates, no touching.
- **Sprint:** flat out against the clock.
- **Head to head:** two rafts, contact allowed.
- **Downriver:** 30 to 60 minutes of endurance.

> [!SAFETY]
> In trouble at the coast? Call 999 or 112 and ask for the coastguard. Inland, on a river or lake, ask for Fire and Rescue. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/water-safety/how-to-call-for-help-at-sea).
