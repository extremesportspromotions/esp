- **What it is:** The award for instructing climbing on single-pitch crags and on climbing walls and towers.
- **What you need first:** You must be 18, be an experienced indoor and outdoor climber and be a member of the BMC, Mountaineering Scotland or Mountaineering Ireland.
- **What you'll learn:** The technical, leadership and teaching skills to run safe single-pitch sessions.
- **How long it takes:** 3 days of training (2 if everyone on the course has done Climbing Wall Instructor training) and 2 days of assessment. Mountain Training says the average gap is 1.1 years.
- **Cost:** Registration is £69. A typical UK course costs £450 to £1,000, with Rock Climbing Instructor training and assessment at the lower end.
- **What's next:** [Climbing coaching awards](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/climbing-coaching-awards-uk) (climbing track). Heading for multi-pitch and mountain crags? [Mountaineering and Climbing Instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/mountaineering-and-climbing-instructor-uk) also needs the Mountain Leader award.

Sun on the crag, ropes rigged and a group about to touch real rock for the first time. That's your office with this award.

## Before training

- 15 led trad routes.
- 15 led routes at a climbing wall.
- 5 led outdoor sport routes.

## Before assessment

- Leading Severe confidently on outdoor crags, on gear you place yourself.
- 40 led trad climbs at a variety of venues, at least 20 of them at Severe or above.
- 30 wall leads at F4 or above, and 10 sport leads at F4 or above.
- Helping to supervise 20 instructed sessions (10 on indoor walls and 10 on outdoor crags), with your own reflective comments on at least five of them in DLOG.
- A current first aid certificate from an in-person course of at least two full days (16 hours) with an assessment.

Our advice: the Rock Skills Learn to Lead Trad course is a solid stepping stone, and Mountain Training points people heading for this award towards it.

> [!SAFETY]
> At the crag, watch for loose rock and falling objects, and keep your helmet on.
