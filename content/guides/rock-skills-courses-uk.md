- **What it is:** Four Mountain Training courses that turn you into an independent rock climber.
- **What you need first:** Nothing for the Introduction course (minimum age 10). Each later course asks for logged climbs and belays (see below).
- **What you'll learn:** Belaying, abseiling, building anchors, seconding, then leading on bolts and on your own gear.
- **How long it takes:** 1 day for the Introduction course, then 2 days each for the other three.
- **Cost:** Set by each provider. A coached outdoor intro climb is typically £55 to £150 per person in the UK.
- **What's next:** [Climbing Wall Instructor](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/climbing-wall-instructor-uk) (climbing track)

Real rock is a different game from the wall. It's cold under your fingertips, it smells of moss and the view behind you is huge. These courses get you there properly.

## The four steps

- **Introduction (1 day, age 10+):** harness on, tie in, belay, abseil and move on real rock. You don't need any experience.
- **Intermediate (2 days, age 12+):** protection, anchors, holding falls, lowering and seconding. You'll need the Introduction course, or 10 routes climbed and 10 belays.
- **Learn to Lead Sport Climbs (2 days, age 14+):** clipping bolts, checking fixed gear and threading the lower-off. You'll need 20 routes climbed and 20 belays.
- **Learn to Lead Trad Climbs (2 days, age 14+):** carrying, choosing and placing your own gear, and building anchors. You'll need 20 belays plus 20 climbs seconded at VDiff or above, or 20 sport leads.

All four run on single-pitch crags across the UK and Ireland.

> [!SAFETY]
> Check your partner's knot and harness, and let them check yours, before every climb. Every time.
