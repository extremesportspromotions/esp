- **What it is:** Skateboard GB's next coaching course after Get Rolling.
- **What you need first:** Get Rolling plus at least 200 hours of teaching since you passed it. Skateboard GB also welcomes experienced coaches who haven't done Get Rolling but have years of on-the-job experience.
- **How long it takes:** Two days, mixing virtual reflection, practical coaching and classroom sessions.
- **Cost:** Skateboard GB lists £400 for Coaching Skills, and coach membership is £50 a year (checked October 2026).
- **What's next:** Coaching track: run lessons, deliver the [Cruiser Award](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skateboard-gb-cruiser-award-uk) or set up a skate school. Riding track: [competitions](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skateboarding-competitions-uk).

## What's on the course

Skateboard GB built this with the coaching community to make coaches more adaptable and creative. Topics include:

- how skaters learn, including cognitive load theory;
- the biopsychosocial model;
- coaching styles, from coach-led to student-led;
- professional judgement and decision making;
- learning design and nested planning;
- reflective techniques.

It draws on what coaches on the course already know and do, so you share ideas with the group.

## Coach membership

If you teach skateboarding, Skateboard GB's Coach Membership is £50 a year. To buy it you need a Skateboard GB coaching qualification, an up-to-date DBS check (or the home nation version) and a safeguarding qualification completed within the last 3 years.

## Running a skate school

Skate School Partner membership is £200 a year. Every coach at the school must have passed a Skateboard GB coaching course, and the school needs at least 3 staff.

## Having a say

The UK Skateboard Coaching Commission brings coaches together, mostly online, to challenge and shape Skateboard GB's coaching work. You don't need lots of qualifications, just real coaching experience.

## Funded places

The DISCO initiative also covers Coaching Skills places for people from marginalised groups.

> [!SAFETY]
> Skateboard GB recommends a helmet every time, because head injuries can cause concussion or death, and it should fit snugly. The NHS says call 999 if someone has hit their head and has been knocked out and not woken up, can't stay awake, has a fit, has fallen from more than 1 metre or 5 stairs, or has problems with their vision or hearing. If they're being sick or feel dizzy, get help from NHS 111. That's from the NHS [head injury and concussion](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) page.
