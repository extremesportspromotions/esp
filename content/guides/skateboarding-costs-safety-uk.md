- **What you need first:** Nothing. No licence is needed to ride.
- **What's next:** Riding track: the [Cruiser Award](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skateboard-gb-cruiser-award-uk), then [competitions](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skateboarding-competitions-uk). Coaching track: [Get Rolling](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skateboard-gb-get-rolling-coach-uk).

## Prices

- **Private lesson:** a one-hour private lesson with a board and pads is usually £32 to £60. Park entry alone isn't a lesson.
- **Private coaching:** usually £32 to £60 an hour.
- **Coaching award:** usually £200 to £450. Skateboard GB lists £200 for Get Rolling and £400 for Coaching Skills (checked October 2026).
- **Skateboard GB membership:** Skateboarder £17.50 a year, Coach £50 a year, Supporter £10 a year (checked October 2026).

## Who's in charge

- **England:** Skateboard GB runs the national coaching awards and competitions, and picks the British Olympic team. It calls itself the governing body for England, but Sport England's list shows no recognised governing body for skateboarding in England.
- **Scotland:** Skateboard Scotland is the recognised governing body in Scotland, named on the sports councils' recognition list.
- **Northern Ireland and Wales:** Skateboard GB works with Skateboard NI, and says a Welsh governing body is soon to be formed. Sport Wales lists no body for skateboarding.

## Kit

- Skateboard GB always recommends a helmet. Pads help, especially wrist guards.
- Buy from a specialist skate shop, not a toy shop.
- Skate shoes, or flat-soled trainers.

## At the skatepark

Skateboard GB says skateparks have an unwritten code of conduct and publishes a [skatepark etiquette guide](https://skateboardgb.org/skate/skateparks-spaces/skateboard-etiquette/). Don't go to a skatepark until you can roll confidently, unless it's for a lesson.

## Welfare

Skateboard GB coaches need an Enhanced DBS check and safeguarding training before they're certified. To report a welfare concern, use its [safeguarding page](https://skateboardgb.org/safeguarding/).

> [!SAFETY]
> Skateboard GB recommends a helmet every time, because head injuries can cause concussion or death, and it should fit snugly. The NHS says call 999 if someone has hit their head and has been knocked out and not woken up, can't stay awake, has a fit, has fallen from more than 1 metre or 5 stairs, or has problems with their vision or hearing. If they're being sick or feel dizzy, get help from NHS 111. That's from the NHS [head injury and concussion](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/head-injury-and-concussion/) page.
