- **What it is:** The first British Skydiving licence. It qualifies you to jump independently.
- **What you need first:** AFF levels 1 to 8 or the Category System to Category 8.
- **What you'll learn:** Stable freefall, controlled deployment and safe landings, plus the canopy theory in the Canopy Training written exam.
- **How long it takes:** At least 10 consolidation jumps after AFF. British Skydiving says many people apply for an A Licence in around 18 jumps.
- **Cost:** Included in the typical AFF price below. British Skydiving membership is extra.
- **What's next:** [The B licence](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-b-licence-uk)

This is the moment it gets real. Consolidation jumps turn what you learned on your course into habits, and every one of them makes you a sharper, calmer skydiver.

## What you have to do

- Finish AFF or the Category System.
- After AFF, make at least 10 consolidation jumps.
- Pass the Canopy Training written exam.

Then you're eligible for your A Licence and cleared to jump on your own or with a coach.

## What it opens up

With an A Licence you can take part in solo and group jumps and start your first discipline sticker, such as FS1 or CT1. See [disciplines and grades](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-disciplines-and-grades-uk).

> [!SAFETY]
> Your canopy flies you home on every jump. Treat the canopy exam as a skill, not a box to tick. In an emergency, call 999.
