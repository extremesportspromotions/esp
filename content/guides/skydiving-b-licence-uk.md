- **What it is:** The second British Skydiving licence.
- **What you need first:** An A Licence, at least 50 jumps, and the JM1 and CT1 qualifications.
- **What you'll learn:** Jump master duties, canopy skills (CT1) and smarter choices about your own kit.
- **How long it takes:** It depends on how often you jump. The minimum is 50 jumps.
- **Cost:** Per coached jump: typical UK price £40 to £80.
- **What's next:** [The C licence](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-c-licence-uk)

Fifty jumps in, you're not a student any more. The B Licence shows you can be trusted with more.

## What you need

- A British Skydiving A Licence.
- At least 50 jumps.
- JM1 and CT1.

## What it lets you do

You can act as jump master, check equipment on the flight line and make more choices about the personal equipment you use. It's also the first licence you need for canopy formation (with at least 100 jumps, 5 of them in the last 2 months) and tracking (with FS1).

Thinking of buying your own rig? British Skydiving says to get advice from your Chief Instructor and check the canopy size chart, Form 330i, before you buy.

> [!SAFETY]
> More freedom means more decisions. A canopy that's too small for your experience is a fast way to get hurt.
