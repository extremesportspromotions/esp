- **What it is:** The third British Skydiving licence.
- **What you need first:** A B Licence, at least 200 total jumps, CT2, one further grade 1 qualification, such as FS1 or FF1, and the C Licence written exam.
- **What you'll learn:** More demanding disciplines, and how to coach newer skydivers.
- **How long it takes:** The minimum is 200 jumps.
- **Cost:** Per coached jump: typical UK price £40 to £80.
- **What's next:** [The D licence](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-d-licence-uk)

Two hundred jumps. You've got the skills, and now the sport starts asking you to give some back.

## What you need

- A B Licence.
- At least 200 total jumps.
- CT2 and one further grade 1 qualification (FS1, FF1 and so on).
- A pass in the C Licence written exam.

## What it lets you do

- Take on more demanding disciplines.
- Wear auxiliary equipment approved by your Chief Instructor, such as a camera helmet.
- Coach less experienced but qualified skydivers in disciplines such as FS1 and FF1.
- Start on the instructor path: Category System Basic Instructor, then Category System Instructor. Tandem Instructor needs 800 jumps and 8 hours of freefall. See [coaching and instructor ratings](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-coach-and-instructor-ratings-uk).

> [!SAFETY]
> A camera is a distraction. Only fly one when your Chief Instructor approves it.
