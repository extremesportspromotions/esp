- **What you need first:** A C Licence and at least 1,000 jumps.
- **Cost:** There's no course price. You get here jump by jump.
- **What's next:** [Disciplines and grades](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-disciplines-and-grades-uk)

A thousand jumps. That's a thousand exits, a thousand openings and a thousand landings. The D Licence says you've lived it.

## What you need

- A C Licence.
- At least 1,000 jumps.

## Where it leads

You need at least a D Licence and ten hours of freefall to become an Accelerated Freefall Instructor. Read more in [coaching and instructor ratings](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skydiving-coach-and-instructor-ratings-uk).

> [!SAFETY]
> Experience is not immunity. Keep doing the same careful kit checks and canopy planning you did on jump one. In an emergency, call 999.
