- **What it is:** Snowsport England's coaching qualifications for competitive and freestyle snowsports.
- **What you need first:** Snowsport England publishes the entry requirements on each course listing.
- **What you'll learn:** Coaching racers, or park, pipe and moguls riders, from club level to elite development.
- **How long it takes:** It depends on the pathway and level.
- **Cost:** Set by Snowsport England. Check the course listing when you book.
- **What's next:** [Competitions](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/snowsport-competitions-uk).

Some riders want gates. Some want rails and kickers. These pathways train the coaches who get them there.

## The pathways

- **Alpine race coaching (Levels 1 to 3):** coaching competitive alpine racers, from club level to elite development.
- **Park and pipe coaching (Levels 1 to 3):** for freestyle skiers and snowboarders.
- **Moguls coaching (Levels 1 and 2).**
- **Nordic:** Roller Ski Instructor and Coach (Levels 1 to 3) and Cross-Country Coach (Levels 2 to 4).

## Your licence

To coach under Snowsport England, you need Snowsport England membership (Red Coach & Instruct, Red Compete or Black), a Snowsport England qualification or approved equivalency, valid first aid, safeguarding training, a DBS check and CPD. You revalidate every year between 1 September and 31 October.

> [!SAFETY]
> Coach within your remit. Snowsport England says you're only insured inside it, and only while your licence is valid.

Source: [Coaching and instructing journey](https://snowsportengland.org.uk/coach-instruct), [Licensing](https://snowsportengland.org.uk/licensing) and [Coaching FAQs](https://snowsportengland.org.uk/coaching-faqs), Snowsport England, checked October 2026.

Governing body: [Snowsport England](https://snowsportengland.org.uk) in England, [Snowsport Scotland](https://www.snowsportscotland.org) in Scotland and [Snowsport Cymru Wales](https://snowsportwales.com) in Wales. They set the standards for skiing and snowboarding, so they're the official places to find out more. Instructor qualifications also come from [BASI](https://basi.org.uk).
