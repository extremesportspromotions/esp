- **What it is:** Competitive skiing and snowboarding, from open-to-all events to the GB teams.
- **What you need first:** Home nation membership for the English Slopestyle Championships (Snowsport England Blue minimum, Snowsport Scotland or Snowsport Cymru Wales).
- **Cost:** The English Slopestyle Championships cost £50 to enter, or £55 on the day, with a lift pass included.
- **What's next:** [Kit, costs and safety](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/snowboarding-kit-costs-safety-uk).

Bib on, music up, one run to land it. You don't need to be elite to compete.

## Where to start

- **English Slopestyle Championships:** an indoor freestyle event each summer, open to all skiers and snowboarders, whatever their age or ability. You need to turn safely down a blue run and use the drag lift. 2027 dates haven't been announced.
- **English Alpine Championships:** Snowsport England's flagship race week in Bormio, Italy, with racers from age eight. In 2027 the FIS races run from 10 to 15 February and the children's races from 14 to 19 February. The race programme is provisional.

- **Scottish Park + Pipe Series:** Snowsport Scotland's four-stop freestyle tour for skiers and snowboarders at Aberdeen, Bearsden, Bellahouston and Hillend, with Grom, Rookie and Adult categories.

## The pathway

Snowsport England runs talent programmes in alpine, park and pipe, para alpine and para snowboard, leading towards GB Snowsport teams. GB Snowsport manages the programme aimed at World Cup podiums and Olympic and Paralympic medals.

For more dates, see our [British competitions 2027](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/british-competitions-2027) guide.

> [!SAFETY]
> Wear a helmet in the park, and inspect the course before you hit it.
