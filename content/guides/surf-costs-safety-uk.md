## Prices

- A two-hour group lesson with kit: £45 to £70.
- A private two hours: £100 to £140.
- Courses, from a block of lessons up to an instructor award: £160 to £400.
- Surfing England's own awards: Surf Instructor: Recreation £300, Surf Coach: Progression £375 (checked October 2026).
- The ISA's online Introduction to Surf Instructing: $100 (checked October 2026).
- Coached time: £35 to £70 an hour.

## Who runs what

- **Surfing England:** the National Governing Body in England, recognised by Sport England and the ISA. It runs instructor and coach awards and accredits surf schools.
- **Welsh Surfing Federation:** the governing body in Wales. Teaching stand-up paddleboarding sits with Canoe Wales.
- **Scottish Surfing:** the governing body for all surfing disciplines in Scotland.
- **GB Surfing:** British teams and elite pathways.
- **ISA:** the world governing body. Its awards are used across the UK.
- **RLSS UK and Surf Life Saving GB:** they run the lifesaving awards coaches need.

## Safety

- Choose a lifeguarded beach. Swimmers go between the red and yellow flags, surfboards between the black and white flags. Never go in under a red flag.
- Rips are strong currents running out to sea. The RNLI says they usually flow at 1 to 2mph but can reach 4 to 5mph, faster than an Olympic swimmer.

> [!SAFETY]
> Caught in a rip? Don't swim against it. Stay with your board, or Float to Live if you don't have one. If you can stand, wade, don't swim. Wade or swim parallel to the shore until you're free, then head for shore. Raise your hand and shout for help. See someone else in trouble? Alert the lifeguards or call 999 or 112 and ask for the coastguard. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/safety/know-the-risks/rip-currents).

From 2028 every sports coach must be on a national coach register. In surfing, that goes through the governing body.
