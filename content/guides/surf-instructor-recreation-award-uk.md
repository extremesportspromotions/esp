- **What it is:** Surfing England's entry-level award (SE1). You teach beginner lessons in waist-deep water to groups of up to 8.
- **What you need first:** Nothing to start. To qualify you need a recognised surf lifesaving award, safeguarding training and the surfing skills below.
- **What you'll learn:** How to coach, plus running safe beginner lessons.
- **Cost:** Surfing England lists £300 (checked October 2026).
- **What's next:** Coach track: [Surf Coach: Progression](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/surf-coach-progression-award-uk). Work track: [Surf school accreditation](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/surf-school-accreditation-uk).

This is where paid surf teaching starts in England.

## What you need to pass

You can start the course straight away. To finish and be certified, you need:

- Paddle out and turtle roll.
- Catch green waves, bottom turn and trim left and right.
- Sign the coaches' code of conduct (it's in the online course).
- An up-to-date beach lifeguard or surf lifesaving award. See [surf lifesaving awards](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/surf-lifesaving-awards-for-coaches-uk).
- Safeguarding training: Surfing England's Surf Safeguarding course (included with this award) or the NSPCC Safeguarding in Sport module. The instructor page calls this "coming soon", but Surfing England's safeguarding page already makes it a prerequisite for all its awards, and you need it for coach membership.

## Age

- At 16 you can be a junior instructor, supervised by an adult surf instructor.
- At 18 you can be a surf instructor.

## How it runs

- Theory online at your own pace, or 2 classroom days with a Trainer Assessor at their surf school.
- About 20 hours of trained and mentored practice on the beach.
- A final assessment.

A disabled trainee who can't meet every lifesaving or demo requirement can qualify as a Supported Surf Instructor. They lead by voice, with a colleague holding a surf lifesaving award, and the ratio changes from 1:8 to 2:8.

To work as a surf coach in England you also keep up an annual Surfing England coach membership. From 2028, Surfing England puts its coach members on the national coach register.

Already hold ISA Level 1? You can get this award through Recognised Prior Learning. See [ISA Level 1](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/isa-level-1-surf-instructor-uk).

> [!SAFETY]
> Caught in a rip? Don't swim against it. Stay with your board, or Float to Live if you don't have one. If you can stand, wade, don't swim. Wade or swim parallel to the shore until you're free, then head for shore. Raise your hand and shout for help. See someone else in trouble? Alert the lifeguards or call 999 or 112 and ask for the coastguard. That's the [RNLI's advice](https://rnli.org/safety/know-the-risks/rip-currents).
