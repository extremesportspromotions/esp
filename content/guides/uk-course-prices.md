We searched course prices at clubs and schools across the UK, so you know what to expect. Typical ranges, checked October 2026. Each club or coach sets its own price.

**We don't take payment for any of these courses.** You book and pay the club or coach directly. [Our match fee](#our-match-fee) pays for our know-how: we find you the right qualified coach for your sport, so you save time and learn from the pros.

## [Mountaineering, coasteering and rock climbing](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/mountaineering-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £55 to £150 per person
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £450 to £1,000 (Rock Climbing Instructor to Mountain Leader; registration extra)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £40 to £70 an hour (outdoor coach)

## [Scuba diving](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/first-scuba-course)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £55 to £160 per person
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £445 to £900 (PADI or SSI Open Water)
- **Per dive:** Typical UK price: £65 to £120 a dive

## [Paragliding](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/learn-to-paraglide-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £140 to £230 per person (BHPA day membership sometimes extra)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £1,250 to £1,700 (Elementary Pilot + Club Pilot; BHPA membership extra)
- **Per day:** Typical UK price: £140 to £200 a day

## [Mountain biking](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/learn-to-mountain-bike-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £80 to £250 per person
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £250 to £600 (British Cycling coaching award)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £45 to £70 an hour

## [Wakeboarding](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/wakeboarding-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £70 to £165 per person (coached cable session with kit)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £595 to £850 (BWSW Level 1 and 2, extras included)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £70 to £165 an hour (coached cable time)

## [Skydiving and indoor skydiving](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/first-tandem-skydive-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £189 to £349 per person (video extra)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £1,400 to £1,900 (AFF + A licence; British Skydiving membership extra)
- **Per coached jump:** Typical UK price: £40 to £80 a coached jump

## [Motocross](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/motocross-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £189 to £350 per person (bike and kit included)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £425 to £560 (one-to-one development day)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £70 to £140 an hour (coached track time with a bike)

## [Kiteboarding](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/kiteboarding-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £130 to £199 per person
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £360 to £1,000 (beginner to independent rider)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £65 to £80 an hour (private lesson)

## [Wingsuit flying](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/wingsuit-flying-for-beginners-uk)

- **First-flight course (after at least 200 skydives):** Typical UK price: £250 to £600 per person (jump tickets extra)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £6,000 to £12,000 from no jumps (AFF, at least 200 jumps, a first-flight course and a wingsuit)
- **Per coached jump:** Typical UK price: £40 to £100 a coached jump

## [Skateboarding and BMX](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/skateboarding-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £32 to £60 per person (one-hour private lesson with board and pads)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £200 to £450 (coaching award)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £32 to £60 an hour (private coaching)

## [Surfing](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/surfing-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £45 to £140 per person (two-hour lesson with kit, group to private)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £160 to £400 (block of lessons to ISA Level 1 surf coach)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £35 to £70 an hour

## [BASE jumping](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/base-jumping-for-beginners-uk)

- **Become qualified:** Typical price abroad: £1,250 to £2,500 (the first BASE course is the entry step; there is no UK qualification)
- **Per day:** Typical price abroad: £200 to £400 a day for coaching

BASE is for experienced skydivers only. You'll need at least 200 skydives, including some from a hot air balloon, before a BASE course will even consider you.

## [Snowboarding and skiing](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/beginners-guide-to-snowboarding-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £70 to £140 per person (private hour or indoor learn-to-ride day)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £600 to £900 (BASI Level 1, up to an indoor package)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £45 to £110 an hour (private lesson)

## [Kayaking and white water rafting](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/kayaking-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £45 to £90 per person
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £220 to £500 (Paddle UK instructor to White Water Leader)
- **Hourly:** Typical UK price: £35 to £60 an hour with a coach

## [Hang gliding](https://www.extremesportspromotions.com/guides/hang-gliding-for-beginners-uk)

- **Experience day:** Typical UK price: £180 to £220 per person (one-day taster)
- **Become qualified:** Typical UK price: £1,300 to £1,600 (Elementary Pilot + Club Pilot; BHPA membership extra)
- **Per day:** Typical UK price: £140 to £200 a day

You pay these prices to the club or coach. Our match fee is separate and paid on top.
