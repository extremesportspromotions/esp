Wakeboarding is riding a board while a boat or a cable pulls you along the water. A beginner starts with that basic ride, not with a jump, a kicker, a ramp or a rail.

## A first session

British Water Ski & Wakeboard (BWSW) is the UK governing body for water skiing and wakeboarding, behind a boat and on a cable. The UK government recognises it as a national governing body.

BWSW describes a beginner's first few sessions on a cable as a lesson on land, a deep-water start on a straight-line cable or a dock start on a full cable, the correct riding position, heel and toe edging, and the corners.


## What to take

Take swimming clothes, a towel and a change of clothes. BWSW says you will be provided with a wetsuit, in a range of sizes, a wakeboard, a helmet if one is needed, and a buoyancy aid or a life jacket.

> [!SAFETY]
> On a site that BWSW accredits, you are given a safety briefing, and you have to wear a helmet and other protective equipment, with a warning that the protection is limited.
