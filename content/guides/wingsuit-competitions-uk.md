## The disciplines

British Skydiving's 2026 Nationals page lists three:

- **Wingsuit Performance:** speed, distance and flight time.
- **Wingsuit Acrobatics:** routines with transitions, carving and acrobatic moves.
- **Tracking Suit:** horizontal movement and speed in a tracking suit.

Performance and Acrobatics each have Rookie, Intermediate and Advanced categories. Rookie is the entry-level one.

## Entry fees

For 2026, British Skydiving listed:

- £45 entry per person, per discipline
- £26 per person per round
- £10 protest fee

That includes a British Team Fund levy of £5 per entry and £1 per paid jump. Re-jumps cost the same as the first jump.

## Before you enter

Entry requirements are in the British Skydiving National Wingsuit Rules. This guide doesn't list them. Read the current rules and next year's event page before you plan.

With relevant CPD, Foundation WS Coaches can coach individuals for Performance and Acrobatic competition. Advanced WS Coaches coach teams and individuals for national and international events.

> [!SAFETY]
> If someone is hurt, call 999. The NHS says 999 is for life-threatening emergencies, and the call handler will want the location (postcode or landmarks), what has happened and your number. That's from the NHS [When to call 999](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/urgent-and-emergency-care-services/when-to-call-999/) page.
