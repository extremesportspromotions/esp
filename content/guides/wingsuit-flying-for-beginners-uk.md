A wingsuit, flown correctly, greatly reduces your vertical speed in exchange for horizontal speed. A first jump is a tandem, a Category System jump or accelerated freefall, and wingsuit flying starts only after many skydives.

## A first session

British Skydiving is the trading name of the British Parachute Association. To begin wingsuit training you must be a C Licence holder with Grade 1 in Tracking, with at least 500 descents, or at least 200 descents in the previous 18 months. You are introduced by a wingsuit coach and you get a full safety brief. Activities such as paragliding, fixed object jumping etc. do not come within the provisions of the British Skydiving Operations Manual. British Skydiving does not associate them with the sport of skydiving. The first-flight manual is for coaches teaching experienced skydivers, and the manual on its own is not enough: you also need instruction from a wingsuit coach.

## What to take

The suit and the rig are chosen with the wingsuit coach. A little too loose is better than too tight, and the coach may refuse the jump if they are not happy with the equipment.
